About Psychedelia 1.4■
Psychedelia 1.4 by Andrew Davidson (1988) — homage.

Choose a pattern, then move the mouse. The pattern animates in B&W around your cursor.

1-9A-D = pattern   C = clear   Click = menu   H = help

Original: 68K Mac, System 1.0 – 9.2. This remake keeps the 1-bit look: black on white, 512×342 spirit, 13 animated patterns.

move mouse to paint • click canvas = back to menu • keys 1-D switch
